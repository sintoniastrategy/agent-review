The human selects what to fix and when to switch from discussion to implementation. Normally handle a complex critical issue individually or a few straightforward fixes together. Three to five is a guideline; a human-approved group of ten or twenty small fixes is valid. Follow repository approval and validation rules.

Use batch-open with approved canonical finding IDs before source edits. The helper saves the before snapshot. Implement only the agreed changes and perform appropriate scoped checks. Use batch-done with --finding for each actually completed ID, a factual summary and the checks actually performed. The helper saves the after snapshot and diff. If a decision changes mid-batch, update it and adjust the remaining work; do not declare an abandoned fix complete. batch-cancel records cancellation without reverting code.

Work in the main author session by default. Delegating fixes is optional. The human can choose delegation in conversation. You may propose it for substantial independent tasks, but launch subagents only with explicit authorization and when repository instructions permit it. Define each agent's finding IDs, file ownership and expected checks. The main author remains responsible for integration, conflicting edits and reporting the combined result. Do not routinely propose delegation for small fixes.

After a batch, state what changed and what was checked. Recommend continuing the remaining selected critical fixes, discussing more findings, requesting a new review, or stopping as appropriate. Do not launch a reviewer automatically after each batch. One subsequent review can verify multiple batches. When requested, explain the selected reviewer, model, effort and full or changes-only scope before launch; honor choices already made. Explicit rechecks remain separate from your record of completion.
